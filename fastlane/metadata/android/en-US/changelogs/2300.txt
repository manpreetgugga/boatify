Four new worlds with 40 new voyages: the Jungle Delta (mangroves, hippos, a crocodile), Dino Lagoon (sauropods, pterosaurs, a plesiosaur), the Sunken City (columns, galleys, an eel in the gate) and the Ghost Sea (graves, ghost ships, a wraith). New castaways, medals, sails and hull paints. Fireflies and wisps light up the night.
