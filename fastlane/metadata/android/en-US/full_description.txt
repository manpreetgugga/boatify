A squall snapped your oar. Now only the wind can carry you from A to B.

No Paddle is a sailing game about reading the wind. You can't row and you can't sail straight into it. Pick your angle, trim the sail, tack your way upwind and ride the gusts home without capsizing, holing the hull on a reef or getting dragged into a whirlpool.

SAIL LIKE A SAILOR
• Point into the wind and the sail just flaps. Zig-zag upwind about 45° off it, and keep your speed through each tack.
• A beam reach is fastest. Dark, ruffled water is a gust; pale, glassy water is a lull or an island's wind shadow.
• Gusts heel her over. Ease the sail or bear away before you go over.
• Stuck head to wind? Dolphins surface on the side to turn to and swim off the way to go.

TEN VOYAGES
Lost Oar, Beam Reach Bay, Into the Wind, Coral Maze, Whirlpool Strait, Wind Shadow, Storm Front, Kraken Fog, Drift Ice and Pirate Waters. Squalls with rain and lightning, a night fog with something in the water, ice floes drifting across the channel with penguins riding them, and a pirate brig that is fast downwind but can't sail against it. The islands are full of life: goats grazing, parrots in the palms, crabs on the beaches, and sea turtles and seals that slip into the sea as you sail by.

MORE TO SAIL
• A new Daily Voyage every day, the same course for everyone. Sail it on consecutive days to build a streak.
• Open Sea: an endless run east against the clock. Sail through the buoy gates to win back time.
• Regattas: race three rival boats round the marks, from the Club fleet up to the Championship.
• Your skipper walks the deck: to the bow, the mast or the rail, or fishing over the stern, and runs back to the tiller when you steer.
• Castaways clinging to wreckage: heave to beside them and bring them home.
• Four boats in the Shipyard, each with its own speed, pointing, stability and toughness.
• 25 medals, some of them secrets, and sails and hull paints to win with them.
• Race the ghost of your best run.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Touch anywhere and slide your thumb to steer, or use a keyboard. Colour-blind friendly colours, reduced motion, three text sizes, left-handed controls and adjustable steering.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no internet needed. Your progress stays on your phone.
