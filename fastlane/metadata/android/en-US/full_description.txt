A squall snapped your oar. Now only the wind can carry you from A to B.

No Paddle is a sailing game about reading the wind. You can't row and you can't sail straight into it. Pick your angle, trim the sail, tack your way upwind and ride the gusts home without capsizing, holing the hull on a reef or getting dragged into a whirlpool.

SAIL LIKE A SAILOR
• Point into the wind and the sail just flaps. Zig-zag upwind about 45° off it, and keep your speed through each tack.
• A beam reach is fastest. Dark, ruffled water is a gust; pale, glassy water is a lull or an island's wind shadow.
• Gusts heel her over. Ease the sail or bear away before you go over.
• Stuck head to wind? Dolphins surface on the side to turn to and swim off the way to go.

HOME WATERS
Ten voyages: Lost Oar, Beam Reach Bay, Into the Wind, Coral Maze, Whirlpool Strait, Wind Shadow, Storm Front, Kraken Fog, Drift Ice and Pirate Waters. Squalls with rain and lightning, a night fog with something in the water, ice floes drifting across the channel with penguins riding them, and a pirate brig that is fast downwind but can't sail against it. The islands are full of life: goats grazing, parrots in the palms, crabs on the beaches, and sea turtles and seals that slip into the sea as you sail by.

TEN MORE WORLDS, A HUNDRED MORE VOYAGES
• Volcano Isles: smoking cones and lava flows, black basalt, falling ash, pumice rafts, and vents that boil and then blow.
• Harbor City: quays stacked with containers, cranes that swing them out over the water, ferries crossing your course, and dock workers waving from the quays.
• Planet Zeta: a violet sea of crystal isles and fungus forests, giant jellies, eyes on stalks that watch you sail by, and beasts that rise from below.
• Jungle Delta: mangroves, macaws, hippos on the tide and a crocodile in its pool.
• Dino Lagoon: sauropods in the ferns, pterosaurs over red cliffs, giant bones and a plesiosaur.
• Sunken City: drowned streets, broken columns, rowing galleys and an eel in the city gate.
• Ghost Sea: islands of graves, green lanterns, ghost ships in the mist and a wraith.
• Aurora Ice: northern lights, glaciers with polar bears, drifting icebergs and an orca.
• Toy Bay: brick islands, sandcastles, rubber ducks and a jack-in-the-box.
• Sky Sea: cloud banks, floating isles with waterfalls, airships and thunderclouds.

MORE TO SAIL
• A new Daily Voyage every day, the same course for everyone. Sail it on consecutive days to build a streak.
• Open Sea: an endless run east against the clock. Sail through the buoy gates to win back time.
• Regattas: race three rival boats round the marks, from the Club fleet up to the Championship.
• Your skipper walks the deck: to the bow, the mast or the rail, or fishing over the stern, and runs back to the tiller when you steer.
• Castaways clinging to wreckage: heave to beside them and bring them home.
• Five boats in the Shipyard, up to a three-masted galleon, each with its own speed, pointing, stability and toughness.
• 35 medals, some of them secrets, and sails and hull paints to win with them.
• Race the ghost of your best run.
• Earn coins as you sail, and hunt for rare black pearls hidden near whirlpools, krakens and pirates.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Touch anywhere and slide your thumb to steer, pinch to zoom, or use a keyboard. Colour-blind friendly colours, reduced motion, three text sizes, left-handed controls and adjustable steering.

No in-app purchases and no account needed. Your progress stays on your phone.
