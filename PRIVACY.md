# Privacy policy

_No Paddle (Android app `io.github.manpreetgugga.boatify`). Last updated 28 October 2026._

No Paddle collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it makes no network connections: the whole game is inside the app and works offline.

## What the app keeps, and where

- **Your progress and settings** (best times, stars, medals, the boats and gear you've unlocked, your logbook and your settings) are saved on your device only. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- **Device backup.** If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include the game's save in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings; the developer can't see it.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes your progress.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**, for short buzzes when the boat bumps into something or picks something up, or you win a star or a medal (you can turn it off in Settings). No other permissions.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
